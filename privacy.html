Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Please read this before you submit a form. This policy describes how Cassidy Silva, partnered with Chosen Agency, handles information you submit through the forms on cassidysilva.life. It is a general policy for this website. It does not replace the privacy policy of an insurance carrier, Jotform, or any other company you interact with separately. If you later apply for insurance, the carrier will collect and use information under its own privacy notice and application forms.

1. Who we are

Cassidy Silva is a licensed life insurance agent, partnered with Chosen Agency. This website is cassidysilva.life. Cassidy is based in Hilo, Hawaii, and is licensed in California, Washington, Hawaii, Indiana, Ohio, Georgia, and Texas.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to cassidysilva.chosen@gmail.com or by calling or texting 206.486.6135.

2. Which forms this policy covers

This policy covers information submitted through:

  • The contact form on the homepage and on the contact page.
  • The become an agent form.
  • The 3 question quiz, including the contact step at the end of the quiz.
  • The Death Fortune Teller lead form, which submits to Jotform as "Doom Report Leads".
  • The coverage calculator, to the extent described in section 3 below. The calculator itself does not submit a form.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to enter. Our forms do not ask for a Social Security number, bank account number, credit card number, password, or medical records. Please do not include that kind of information in a free text message field.

Contact forms (homepage and contact page). Your name, a phone number or email address, the topic you select (life insurance for you or your family, retirement, annuities or a 401(k) rollover, becoming an agent, or something else), and anything you choose to write in the optional message field.

Become an agent form. Your name, a phone number or email address, the situation you select (career change, side income, already licensed and looking for guidance, or just curious), and anything you choose to write about your biggest question about the business.

Quiz. The answers you select: what you are trying to protect, the state you live in, and your age band (under 30, 30s, 40s, 50s, or 60 plus). At the end of the quiz, if you choose to send your result, we also collect the name and phone number or email address you enter.

Death Fortune Teller lead form. Your first name, last name, email address, and phone number, plus the fortune result shown to you and the time of submission, as recorded by Jotform.

Coverage calculator. The numbers you enter (annual income, years of income to replace, debts, future costs, and coverage or savings you already have) are used in your browser to show an estimate. The calculator itself does not send those numbers to Cassidy. If you then use a contact form or the quiz to follow up, only the information in that form is sent.

When you visit the site, the hosting provider may also log basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance. This website does not use advertising cookies or third party analytics as of the effective date above. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

4. How your information is sent

Most forms on this site use your own email program to send your message. When you press Send, your email program opens with a prefilled message addressed to cassidysilva.chosen@gmail.com. The information is then sent as email, which means it passes through your email provider as well as Cassidy's email provider.

The Death Fortune Teller form is different. It submits directly to Jotform, and a notification is sent to Cassidy's email. Jotform stores the submission under its own systems, subject to Jotform's privacy policy.

Because email is not completely secure, do not send sensitive account or health information through these forms. If you need to share application information later, Cassidy or the carrier will give you a secure way to do it.

5. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your question and follow up about the topic you selected.
  • Provide general education about life insurance, retirement income, annuities, 401(k) rollovers, living benefits, or becoming an agent.
  • Schedule a consultation or mentoring call, if you ask for one.
  • Prepare for a later conversation with you, including remembering what you asked about and which state you live in.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping requirements that apply to insurance agents.

We do not use form submissions to make a coverage decision, set a price, or issue a policy. Any quote or application is a separate step, handled under the carrier's process.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross context behavioral advertising.

We may share information only as follows:

  • Service providers. Jotform, for the Death Fortune Teller form, and email and hosting providers that deliver and store messages for this site. These providers may only use the information to provide their service.
  • Chosen Agency and insurance carriers. Only if you ask Cassidy to follow up, get a quote, or start an application, and only the information needed for that step.
  • Legal compliance. If required by law, regulation, a court order, or a regulator, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Cassidy, Chosen Agency, or others.
  • Business changes. If Cassidy's practice is transferred, the information needed to continue serving you may transfer with it, and you will be notified as required by law.

Links on this site, including Instagram, go to other companies. Their privacy policies apply when you use them.

7. Calls, texts, and emails, and your consent

Submitting a contact form or quiz result is a request for Cassidy to contact you about the topic you selected. It is not, by itself, consent to marketing messages unrelated to that request.

The Death Fortune Teller form includes a separate consent checkbox. By checking that box and submitting the form, you give Cassidy Silva, partnered with Chosen Agency, your prior express written consent to contact you by phone call, text message, and email at the contact details you provided, including by automated dialing or prerecorded messages, about life insurance products and related offers. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time, for example by replying STOP to a text, or by emailing or calling Cassidy using the contact details in section 1.

You may opt out of future marketing contact at any time by replying STOP to a text, or by contacting Cassidy by email or phone. Opting out of marketing does not stop messages needed to complete a conversation or transaction you have already started, or messages required by law.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond to you, follow up on your request, and meet insurance recordkeeping and legal requirements. When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we delete it or deidentify it, subject to any legal hold or retention rule that applies.

If you ask us to delete your information, we will delete it from our active records unless we are required to keep it for legal, regulatory, or recordkeeping reasons. In that case, we will tell you what we must keep and why.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information in our email and form accounts, including access limited to Cassidy and her service providers. No website, email system, or form is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a secure method if you are ever asked to share sensitive application information.

10. Children

This website and its forms are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect insurance information from minors. If you believe a child has submitted information through a form, contact Cassidy using the details in section 1 and we will delete it.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you rights over your personal information, including the right to know what we collect and why, to access a copy of it, to correct inaccurate information, to ask us to delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information, as described in section 6.

To exercise a right, email cassidysilva.chosen@gmail.com or call or text 206.486.6135 and tell us what you are asking for. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request, for example by confirming the email address or phone number you used on the form. You may authorize another person to make a request on your behalf, and we may ask for proof of that authorization. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

California residents may also have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, including the rights described above. Texas residents may have rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, and residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws may have similar rights. This section is a general summary, not a complete statement of any state's law, and it does not create rights beyond what applicable law provides.

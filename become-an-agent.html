Mentorship

Want to become an insurance agent?

I didn’t grow up around money. I grew up being told rich people were greedy and there was no ethical way to make a living in finance. That turned out to be false, and now I teach other people the truth I had to learn the hard way.

Who I mentor: career changers, people looking for honest side income, and new agents who don’t want to become the pushy salesperson everyone dreads. Especially people from backgrounds like mine who want to help their own communities build wealth.

What mentorship covers: getting licensed in your state, your first sales without feeling gross about it, and building a book of clients who actually trust you.

How I get paid as your mentor: ask me. I’ll tell you straight, the same way I tell my insurance clients how I’m compensated. No pitch without the math.

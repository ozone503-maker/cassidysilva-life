My mission

My mission is to help local families avoid the financial devastation of losing someone they love, and to help our kūpuna retire comfortably, often earlier than they expected. Too many of our aunties and uncles are still working in their 70s and 80s, unsure if they will ever be able to stop. I want to help change that.

Why I do this

I am originally from Honolulu. After the 2008 recession, my family faced severe financial hardship. There were days when we did not have enough to eat.

We did not have access to financial education. I grew up with deep distrust of the system, the government, and wealthy people in general. I was taught that there was no ethical way to build wealth or save for retirement.

That is not true. I help families from backgrounds like mine see that it is possible to earn, save, and protect what matters, with integrity.

In 2019, my mom passed away with no life insurance. My siblings and I scrambled to cover final expenses. We had a GoFundMe, called and begged family for help, and tanked our credit scores by maxing out cards and taking out loans we knew we couldn't pay back on time. I do this so other families do not have to go through that.
